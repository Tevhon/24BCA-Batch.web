About This Site

24-BCA Study Hub is a one-stop place built by the batch, for the batch — PYQ papers, notes, assignments and lab experiments in one spot instead of scattered across five different WhatsApp chats.

Why This Exists

Everything in one place

PYQ papers, notes, assignments and lab experiments, organised by subject and semester instead of buried in chat history.

Grade tools that matter

An SGPA and CGPA calculator built specifically around Chandigarh University's 10-point grading scale.

A real community

Batch and coder WhatsApp communities where students actually help each other out before exams.

How It Came Together

01

The problem

Notes and papers were scattered across a dozen WhatsApp chats, hard to dig up right before exams.

02

A single lightweight site — HTML, CSS and JS — hosted for free and updated as the semester goes on.
